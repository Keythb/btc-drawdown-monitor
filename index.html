The composite blends them into a LOW print, and that blend is doing real work the headline number hides. ------------------------------------------------------------ INVESTMENT CONTEXT Category scores and weights: On-Chain 26.8 (40%) Derivatives 34.3 (25%) Technical 34.6 (20%) Macro 41.3 (10%) Sentiment 36.6 (5%) Macro is the highest-scoring pillar at 41.3 but carries only a 10% weight, so its contribution to the composite is roughly 4 points. A contracting Tether float removes the marginal dollar of on-exchange purchasing power. It is a demand-side constraint, not a valuation signal, which is precisely why it sits at 86 while every valuation metric sits below 20. Stablecoin float contraction has historically preceded periods where dips are bought more slowly, extending the duration of drawdowns rather than deepening them. Retail long-short ratio at 2.13 (score 78) is the second concern and reads contrarian-bearish: retail accounts are carrying better than two longs for every short into a tape that is sitting at its 200-week average with Fear and Greed at 27. That combination -- crowded retail longs alongside a fearful headline sentiment print -- is internally inconsistent and usually resolves against the crowded side. Long positioning that thick is the fuel for a liquidation cascade if support gives way; open interest at 6,067 BTC and the liquidation-cluster proxy at 32 suggest the leverage stack is not yet extreme, which caps how violent that cascade could be. Derivatives at 34.3 are otherwise unremarkable. Futures basis at 4.3% annualized is a subdued carry, consistent with modest speculative appetite rather than froth. What would have to change to shift the tier: the composite needs about 2.8 points to cross into MODERATE (35-50). Given the weights, that is roughly a 7-point deterioration in the On-Chain block alone, or a combination such as On-Chain +4 and Derivatives +5. The realistic path is a break below the 200-week average that simultaneously degrades ma_200w_dev, mvrv_z, and nupl -- those three move together and together carry enough weight to reprice the headline in a single session. ------------------------------------------------------------ SCENARIO FRAMEWORK Base case -- range persistence (approx. 55%) Price holds the $58,000-$68,000 band.
