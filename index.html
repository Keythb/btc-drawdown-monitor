TON618 CAPITAL - BITCOIN DRAWDOWN MONITOR Daily Risk Narrative - 2026-08-30 Composite: 39.5/100 [MODERATE] | BTC $78,243.93 ------------------------------------------------------------ EXECUTIVE SUMMARY The composite drawdown-risk reading is 39.5/100, MODERATE, up 2.4 points from 37.1 the prior session. The move is small in absolute terms but it is not evenly sourced: essentially all of the increase comes from the profit- taking and distribution complex on-chain, not from leverage, not from positioning, and not from macro. SOPR printed 1.2365 and scores a maximum 100 - coins are moving on-chain at a materially higher price than they were acquired at, which is the classic signature of realized-profit supply entering the market. Active-address divergence at 82 (35.55) says that supply is not being met by a widening user base. The offsetting fact is that the valuation and structural block remains benign and did not deteriorate: 200-week moving-average deviation scores 14, reserve risk 16, and MVRV Z-score 17 (1.475). Those three are the metrics that historically separate an ordinary distribution episode from a cycle top, and none of them is stressed. That combination - hot flow, cold valuation - is what keeps the composite inside MODERATE rather than pushing it toward ELEVATED. Nothing in today's tape argues for a change in posture. It argues for watching one specific channel. ------------------------------------------------------------ INVESTMENT CONTEXT Category scores and weights: On-Chain 39.6 weight 40% Derivatives 39.6 weight 25% Technical 42.6 weight 20% Macro 32.7 weight 10% Sentiment 39.0 weight 5% The dispersion across categories is unusually tight - a 9.9-point spread between the highest (Technical 42.6) and the lowest (Macro 32.7). Tight dispersion means no single category is carrying the composite and no single category is masking a problem in another. It also means the composite is currently a poor early-warning instrument on its own: a genuine regime change would show up first as one category breaking away from the pack, and that has not happened. On-Chain carries the largest weight at 40% and is the category doing the work today. Derivatives at 39.6 is notable for what it is not doing - open interest scores 30, funding 25, futures basis 25. Leverage is not the story. Funding is mildly negative (-0.000315 over the trailing twelve 8-hour periods), meaning shorts are paying longs on the perpetual, and annualized basis of 4.15% on the September future is unremarkable against cash rates with the effective fed funds at 3.63%. There is no carry excess to unwind. Macro at 32.7 is the lowest category and is genuinely supportive. The 10-year at 4.67% scores 47, fed funds at 3.63% scores 22, the dollar proxy at 98.9 scores 34, and 30-day SPX correlation is 0.093 - scoring 22. That last figure matters: at effectively zero correlation, bitcoin is not currently a levered expression of equity beta, which removes one of the main historical channels through which an external shock transmits into a bitcoin drawdown. ------------------------------------------------------------ RISK ANALYSIS Primary risk drivers, ranked: sopr 100 1.2365 aad 82 35.55 bollinger_width 81 41.05 fear_greed 68 69 iv_rv 66 0.777 The three that matter are the top three, and they are telling a coherent single story rather than three independent ones. SOPR at 1.2365 means spent outputs are realizing an average 24% gain. A sustained reading at this level is supply pressure by definition - it is holders converting unrealized gain into realized gain, which requires a bid to absorb. It is not, by itself, a top signal; SOPR runs hot through the majority of any advance. It becomes a warning when it stays elevated while the absorbing bid thins. Active-address divergence at 82 is the measure of whether that bid is thinning. Price and network usage are diverging - the distribution described by SOPR is not being met by proportional new on-chain activity. This is the single most important pairing in today's reading. SOPR alone is normal. SOPR at 100 plus AAD at 82 is a marginal-buyer question. Bollinger width at 81 (41.05) is a compression reading, not a direction. It says the recent realized range has narrowed relative to its own history. Compression is symmetric - it resolves violently in whichever direction it resolves - but it is a risk-amplifier rather than a risk-source: it raises the magnitude of the next move without saying anything about the sign. Sentiment is warm but not extreme. Fear-greed at 69 sits in greed territory without reaching the 80-plus zone where it becomes a contrarian input on its own. Retail long-short ratio at 1.17 scores 39 - retail is net long, mildly, not crowdedly. The options market is the notable dissent from the on-chain warning. IV/RV at 0.777 (ATM implied 34.5 against realized 44.4) scores 66 and is flagged as a driver, but the direction of that read deserves care: implied vol is trading BELOW realized. Options are cheap relative to what the spot tape has actually delivered. Put-call ratio at 0.562 scores 55 - calls outnumber puts on open interest. The 25-delta skew is -1.36, with call IV at 35.2 against put IV at 36.5 - a mild put bias in pricing, but only mild. The derivatives complex is not paying up for downside protection. Either the options market is underpricing the distribution risk the on-chain data is flagging, or the on-chain data is describing routine profit-taking that will be absorbed. That disagreement is the most interesting feature of today's reading, and it is cheap to express a view on it while implied sits under realized. Support levels holding the composite down: ma_200w_dev 14 1.214 reserve_risk 16 0.31 mvrv_z 17 1.474997 rsi_divergence 22 0 spx_correlation 22 0.093 Price at 1.214x the 200-week moving average and an MVRV Z-score of 1.475 are both far from the zones that have preceded major drawdowns. Reserve risk at 0.31 says long-term-holder conviction relative to price is not stretched. RSI divergence is flat at zero - momentum is confirming price rather than diverging from it. ------------------------------------------------------------ SCENARIO FRAMEWORK Base case - composite holds 35-50 (MODERATE) over the next 5-10 sessions. Probability approximately 60%. SOPR mean-reverts toward 1.05-1.15 as the current profit-taking cohort is absorbed, active addresses stabilize, and the Bollinger compression resolves without a range expansion large enough to move the Technical block. In this path spot chops within roughly 8-10% of $78,244 - call it $71,000 to $86,000 - and no category breaks away. Escalation - composite crosses 50 into ELEVATED. Probability approximately 25%. The trigger is specific and observable: SOPR staying above 1.20 for another three-plus consecutive sessions WHILE active-address divergence holds above 75. That is the marginal-buyer failure case - continued distribution into a shrinking user base. Secondary confirmation would come from the Bollinger compression resolving downward with an expansion in realized volatility, which would push the Technical score from 42.6 toward the mid-50s. On-Chain at 40% weight plus Technical at 20% is 60% of the composite; both moving together is the only realistic path from 39.5 through 50 in under two weeks. A drawdown consistent with that configuration is on the order of 15-25% from current levels, taking spot to roughly $59,000- $66,000, with the 200-week average - currently implied near $64,400 by the 1.214 deviation - the structural reference. De-escalation - composite falls back below 35 into LOW. Probability approximately 15%. Requires SOPR to reset through 1.0, meaning the profit- taking cohort is exhausted and coins begin moving at or below cost basis, alongside active-address recovery pulling AAD under 50. Macro at 32.7 is already supportive and would need only to stay there. This is the path where the compression resolves upward on genuine new participation rather than on leverage. Tail - a fast, correlated risk-asset shock. Currently the least likely transmission channel and deliberately not weighted into the above. SPX correlation at 0.093 means bitcoin is not presently moving as an equity proxy; that decoupling would need to re-establish first, and re-coupling is observable in advance in the correlation series. Watch that number more than any headline. ------------------------------------------------------------ RECOMMENDATION Maintain current exposure. No de-risking action is warranted at 39.5. The composite is mid-range, the valuation block is benign, and the leverage complex is not stressed - the three conditions that would normally justify reducing are absent. This is an observation about relative pricing, not a directional call. Do not act on the Bollinger compression alone.
